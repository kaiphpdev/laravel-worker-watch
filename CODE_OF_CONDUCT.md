# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- give constructive technical feedback;
- focus discussion on the code, design, documentation, and project goals;
- respect differing experience levels and perspectives;
- accept reasonable maintainer decisions about project scope and direction.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory or degrading comments;
- deliberate disruption of project discussions;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- repeated hostile or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or restrict contributions and participation that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately rather than escalating the matter in a public thread.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to official project spaces and to public interactions directly representing the project.
